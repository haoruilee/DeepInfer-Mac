\section*{Reproducibility statement}
The engine, converter and tests are released under the Apache 2.0 license at \url{https://github.com/haoruilee/DeepInfer-Mac}. Section~\ref{sec:impl} and the appendix describe the weight format, and \texttt{python -m deepinfer\_mac convert} rebuilds the checkpoint from the public release. The parity tests in \texttt{tests/} run against the official reference implementation when its source is available through \texttt{DSV41\_REF}, and are skipped otherwise.

Every measurement in the paper comes from a script in \texttt{scripts/}, and its raw output is stored in \texttt{paper/logs}. The schedule ablation and the cache sweep come from \texttt{spec\_ablation.py} and \texttt{spec\_sweep.py}, the timelines from \texttt{trace\_decode.py}, and the SSD and prefill measurements from \texttt{bench\_io\_parallel.py} and \texttt{prefill\_overlap.py}. Earlier decode measurements used \texttt{decode\_speed.py}, \texttt{spec\_ab.py} and \texttt{schedule\_ab.py}, and the replacement-policy replay used \texttt{trace\_policies.py}. Output quality comes from \texttt{score\_api.py}, \texttt{real\_check.py} and \texttt{quant\_damage.py}, and the indexer study from \texttt{capture\_indexer.py}, \texttt{dosa\_study.py} and \texttt{bench\_kernels.py}. The captured indexer inputs, about 300\,MB, are not included.

One interleaved ablation run, stored as \texttt{ablation2.json}, was disturbed by an external event that slowed every setting to below a tenth of a token per second in some blocks; we repeated it and do not use it. The quality numbers come from the v0.1.1 build, whose log is \texttt{final\_eval.log}; a later build scored slightly higher, as \texttt{after\_score.log} shows. \texttt{paper/figures/make\_plots.py} draws all figures from \texttt{paper/figures/data} and from values copied out of these logs.
